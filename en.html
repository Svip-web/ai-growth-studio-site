Frequently asked questions

How long does content creation take?

We usually share initial concepts within 2–3 days. The full timeline depends on the scope and number of formats.

Can you create a complete series for one product?

Yes. For example, one skincare range can become a cohesive series of catalogue and lifestyle images, ad creatives, Reels and social media content.

Can AI content be used in advertising?

Yes, provided the assets comply with the relevant platform requirements and applicable law. We recommend agreeing on the format and intended use when defining the brief.

Can I order just photography or just video?

Yes. You do not need to order a complete package.

What do you need from the client?

Usually, product information, photos, a logo and any existing brand assets are enough. We agree on anything else at the start of the project.

Can you retain my existing brand style?

Yes. We can work with your brand colours, typography, visual guidelines and existing materials.

How does the approval process work?

We first agree on the direction and references, then share work in progress and refine the chosen solution.
